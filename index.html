How It Works

1. Drag & Drop

Select files or folders and drag them into Keka. Choose your compression format—7z, Zip, or others.

2. Compress Instantly

Hit compress. Keka optimizes and creates your archive in seconds, with full sandbox compatibility.

3. Extract Effortlessly

Drop any supported archive—RAR, ISO, EXE, and more. Extract to your chosen location with one click.

4. Done

Your files are ready. No setup, no fuss—pure efficiency.
